PRD — Lopa
AI-Powered Digital Wellbeing Platform for Windows

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft
Product: Lopa

1. Executive Summary

Lopa is a premium AI-powered digital wellbeing platform designed for Windows.

The product combines:

realtime activity tracking
behavioral analytics
AI-generated insights
focus management
emotionally intelligent UX
luxury glass-based UI

Unlike traditional productivity software, Lopa is positioned as:

“A digital wellbeing operating layer.”

The platform prioritizes:

privacy transparency
beautiful design
low system resource usage
scalable architecture
AI-enhanced habit intelligence

Create the most visually refined and emotionally intelligent digital wellbeing platform for desktop operating systems.
